Lesson 1: Basic and Assignment Operators

Did you know you could do math in Java too? Remember your basic arithmetic from school for one second. We have addition, subtraction, multiplication, etc. They all also follow the rules of BEDMAS (brackets, divison/multiplication, addition/subtraction). Where it differs is in division and the introduction of modulus.

DIf you are dividing integers, they will do something called truncation to the decimal. This is removing the decimal (i.e. 13 / 2 would result in 6 rather than 6.5). Float division will preserve the decimal, however. Modulus uses the percentage symbol and allows you to get the remainder of a division of numbers (i.e. 6 % 5 would result in 1). Modulus is calculated at the same time as multiplication and division in BEDMAS.

Operator Symbol Example
Addition + a = 6 + 7;
Subtraction - b = 7 - 6;
Multiplication * c = 6 * 7;
Integer Division / d = 16 / 4;
Float Division / e = 16.7 / 4.3;
Modulus % f = 15 % 2;
