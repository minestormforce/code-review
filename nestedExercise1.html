Exercise 1

Make a movie ticket price checker. The program will ask for three inputs: age, the day of the week, and for promo codes. The base ticket costs $20. If they are under 13 or over 69, they get a 10% discount. If the day of the week is Saturday, they get an additional 10|% off. If they enter the promocode “Discount123,” they also get an additional 10% off. Each discount is additive with one another. And don’t forget error checking!!!
