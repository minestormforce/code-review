Lesson 1: Syntax and Logic Errors

As you’re on your coding journey, you have probably encountered errors before. Whether it be an error stopping your program from running, to an error that gives you the wrong output. The former is known as a syntax error, and the latter is known as a logic error.

Syntax errors happen during compile-time. The compiler will give you a warning that there is something wrong with your code and many IDEs will tell you what is wrong with your program. These are usually caused by typos or simply forgetting the semi-colon after declaring a variable.

Logic errors are much harder to find. The program will run, but will end up giving the wrong output. You can only find these errors by testing the program out and the debugging, which is the process of looking for and fixing errors in your code.
