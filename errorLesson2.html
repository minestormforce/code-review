Lesson 2: Style Guide

YStyle is important for your code as it makes it easier for others to understand, and easier for you to understand when you’re debugging. This is something that should become a habit when you write programs. Using camel case when naming variables is an example of good style.

You should try to implement comments in your code. There are two types of comments: single-line and multi-line (the image below has shows syntax for both). Comments should be understood by other people reading it and should be as concise as possible. You do not have to comment absolutely everything, but try to consider someone else reading your code, where would comments help clarify your code?

Blank space or whitespace is also a good habit to have, you shouldn’t have all your code mashes up into as few lines as possible. Try to separate chunks of code that work together with blank lines. Indentation can also help in heiarchies, and is especially useful in nested statements (more in Unit 5).
