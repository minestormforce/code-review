Lesson 1: Statements and Expressions

A statement is a line of code and in Java, most statements end with a semi-colon. Examples are declaring variables, assigning values, etc. An expression is a statement that either be reduced to a value, or assign a value. There are two types of expressions: numerical and logical. Numerical expressions combine numbers or variables and mathematical operators. Logical expressions combine numbers or variables and logical operators.
